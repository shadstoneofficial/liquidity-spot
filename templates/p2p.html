How Alice & Bob Work Here

  • Alice is always the seller of HNS.
  • Bob is always the buyer of HNS.
  • If an offer says Sell HNS, the offer creator is Alice.
  • If an offer says Buy HNS, the offer creator is Bob.

Why P2P Is Here

  • Shared trade room and milestone tracking
  • TXID submission, notes, and confirmations
  • Disputes and moderation support
  • No platform custody of funds
