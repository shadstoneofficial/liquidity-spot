Login With GFAVIP

GFAVIP login unlocks account features on `Liquidity.spot`.

You can browse public P2P offers, create offers, accept offers, and enter trade rooms without logging in. GFAVIP is optional and adds account-linked benefits like Gems, saved activity, and reputation.

What GFAVIP Adds

  • Single sign-on for your GFAVIP identity
  • Account tier sync for optional account features
  • Gems balance sync for marketplace features
  • Cleaner wallet-service integration for future bond enforcement

What Happens Next

  • You go to GFAVIP to authenticate
  • GFAVIP redirects you back to `Liquidity.spot`
  • Your username, tier, and Gems balance are synced into your account here
Continue With GFAVIP

Note: GFAVIP is optional for normal P2P use. It is only required when you choose features that depend on your GFAVIP account, such as Gems or account-linked history.
